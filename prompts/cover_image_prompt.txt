Main title (RENDER THIS TEXT clearly and legibly on the cover):

Session content (2–4 paragraphs — each paragraph represents one topic

CANVAS SAFE ZONE (most important rule — get this wrong and the
cover is unusable):
- Treat the outer 10% of every edge as a SAFE MARGIN. No part of any
  letter may touch or extend into this margin.
- The main title must sit fully inside the inner 80% of the canvas.
- If the title is long, REDUCE the font size or wrap onto multiple
  lines so EVERY letter stays inside the safe zone. Never crop or
  truncate the title to make it fit — resize it.
- Specifically: NO letter of the first or last word may be cut off
  by the left or right edge of the image.

Composition:
- Main title in the LEFT HALF of the canvas, vertically centred,
  inside the safe zone. Elegant, refined typography. Crisp, readable,
  no decorative flourishes.
- RIGHT HALF: one small icon or minimal illustration per paragraph,
  stacked vertically with equal spacing, each visually representing
  the key theme of that paragraph. Icons should be clean, modern,
  corporate-style — line art or flat illustration. Each icon sits
  inside a subtle rounded card or neutral container. Generous negative
  space between icons. Do NOT merge them into a single scene.
- Soft neutral cream background. Refined teal, sage, cream, and
  warm beige accent palette.
- Tell the story visually through the per-paragraph icons; keep each
  one simple and immediately readable at a glance.

Mood: confident, elegant, calm. Editorial-quality, polished.

Strict rules:
- Render the main title text EXACTLY as provided. Do not paraphrase,
  shorten, or add words.
- No subtitles, captions, footers, watermarks, or any text other than
  the main title.
- ABSOLUTELY NO LOGOS, brand marks, mascots, or company emblems of
  any kind — no Livestorm logo, no fictional logos, no abstract
  marks that read as a logo. The cover is unbranded.
- Avoid faces or identifiable people.
- Avoid charts, numbers, dashboards.
