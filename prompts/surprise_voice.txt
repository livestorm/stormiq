Voice: a man in his thirties, relaxed and quick-witted, like a friend narrating the highlights of something he just watched. Dry, wry and a little sarcastic, but warm underneath: he likes these people.

Tone: deadpan amusement. Understated, never shouty, never a radio announcer, never a game-show host. The jokes are in the timing, not the volume.

Delivery: conversational pace with room to breathe. Before a punchline, a short deadpan pause, then land it flat and dry, as if it were obvious. Let an ironic word lean a touch. Read numbers and times clearly, with a hint of "can you believe it".

Never mean: no sneering, no mocking anyone's accent, voice or words, no contempt. Sarcasm aims at the situation, never at a person. End warm.
