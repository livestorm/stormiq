Write in {output_language}, in Markdown, with exactly these four sections, in this order, and no other heading:

## What happened
3 to 5 sentences: what the session was about, how it was organised (use the chapters, with their times, when there are some), and its main message. Plain prose, no bullets.

## What landed with the audience
3 to 5 bullets. Each ties an audience reaction (a chat peak, a burst of questions, a prompt the room answered or ignored, a time in the room) to the moment of the talk that caused it, with its time. Use only the numbers and moments in the brief. On an internal meeting, the audience is the team.

## Questions
2 to 5 bullets: what the audience asked about most, what was answered (in writing, aloud, or in the talk, with the time), and which questions are still open. Name the open ones plainly, so someone can answer them.

## What to do next
3 to 5 bullets, each a concrete action for the team, tied to something in the brief (an open question to answer, a figure to check before sharing, a part of the talk to cut or expand next time, a moment worth reusing as a clip with its time).
