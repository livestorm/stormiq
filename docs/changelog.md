# Changelog

What has shipped, newest first. What's next is in [roadmap.md](roadmap.md).

## November 2026: "StormIQ for Livestorm"

- **Surprise me is a show, made in one click.** "Generate the show" writes the take, turns it into a short spoken stand-up bit (fillers, a pause before the twist, sarcastic about situations and never about people, every figure the take's), plans the scenes and records the voice, behind one loader; a take written before gets "Make the show". The player now comes first and big, and the take sits behind "Show transcript" with Copy and Download (.md). The voice is ElevenLabs (Finley, a deep British voice, by default; Chris or any voice id in Admin), with OpenAI's cedar as the backup when ElevenLabs can't be used, which the show then says. The spoken script is only heard: CC shows the take's own sentence. No crowd sounds.
- **Follow-up's list folds.** Its groups fold, with only the one on screen open and the others showing what's ready, and Surprise me moved out of the list into its own card at the top.
- **Surprise me tells a story.** The take may now quote the room: a few of the chat's most telling messages and the top questions (most upvoted first) are given to it, redacted and never with who wrote them, to use only when they make the joke better. "Play it" no longer writes the sentence out: each beat is a storyboard scene, a few of its own words slammed in, stamped on or popped in word by word, a number it says counting up, a chat message or a question flying in as a bubble, the chat's curve drawing to its peak, a flash of colour on a punchline. Claude plans the scenes on the first Play and code checks every word against the beat; "CC" shows the sentence as a subtitle, off by default. The voice is now a man, cedar, dry and a little sarcastic, warm underneath, never mean; old takes record again on their next Play ([product.md](product.md#the-follow-up-tab-v1)).
- **Surprise me is opt-in.** It is now off for every organization except StormIQ's own; an admin turns it on for another one in Admin → Organizations.
- **Follow-up's list stays in view.** The list of outputs on the left keeps its place and scrolls on its own, and picking an output while scrolled down takes you to its top.
- **Surprise me, played with a voice and the session's own data.** "Play it" now reads each sentence with an AI voice (OpenAI, marin by default; Admin picks another) and shows it as a scene drawn from the session: the chat's curve lighting up the minute a sentence names, a ring for a percentage, a number counting up, a bold card for a punchline, the captions revealed word by word with the voice. The first Play records the voice (paid, in the worker, from the take's text only) behind the usual loader; then it is kept, so replays are free. "Play with captions only" needs no voice. Controls for previous, next, pause, from the start and sound off; it works on a phone and with reduced motion ([product.md](product.md#the-follow-up-tab-v1)).
- **The FAQ, edited in place.** Follow-up's FAQ is now a list you edit: StormIQ drafts it from the question desk (no AI), one question per entry with its answer from Livestorm, from your reply on the desk, or from the talk with its time, and attendees' names, emails and links already removed. Edit any question or answer, add, remove or reorder them, then save. Answers you add on the desk afterwards are offered ("1 question newly answered on the desk · Add them"), never written over yours. Copy it as Markdown, or download it as Markdown, CSV or PDF. English only for now; the FAQ the AI wrote before stays readable under "Earlier AI version" ([product.md](product.md#the-follow-up-tab-v1)).
- **Follow-up's outputs show the loader too.** Writing an output (a Smart Recap, the FAQ, the digest…) now shows the same steps, bar and clock as the rest of the app, instead of "Writing: 40%", and the output being written shows a small spinner in the list on the left.
- **Surprise me, played.** A surprise take now has "Play it": the take one sentence at a time in large type, read aloud by your browser's voice, with its numbers counting up, its key words marked and each punchline popping in after a pause. It runs in the page, free; nothing is sent anywhere.
- **One loader for every job.** The Report's analysis card and the Transcript tab's transcript now show the same loader as a session being prepared: the steps with the current one's detail, a bar that fills with the job's real progress, and the time so far. Follow-up's outputs come next.
- **Surprise me is refused less often.** Its check now reads numbers said aloud (the transcript often spells them, "fifteen" or "vingt-cinq", where the take writes 15 or 25), and a take that still gives a figure the session doesn't have is written once more, told why, before the page shows an error.
- **Attendee follow-up ("You asked, we answered").** Follow-up has a new group, For the audience, with its first format: the email to send attendees after the session, with what it covered, three takeaways and two of their questions answered, and a `{{replay_link}}` for your email tool. Sonnet 5.5 writes it on click, on every session, from the chapters, the key moments of the talk and two answered questions from the question desk. Attendees' names, emails and links are removed before the AI writes, and StormIQ saves nothing that gives a number or a time the session doesn't have. French is a translation of the English one ([ai-outputs.md](ai-outputs.md#attendee-follow-up)).
- **Surprise me can be turned off for an organization.** Admin has a new Organizations tab listing every organization with its sessions and a switch for Surprise me, for customers who ask (a regulated industry, a formal brand). Off, it disappears from Follow-up and the server refuses a take, also for a job already queued ([data-model.md](data-model.md#org_settings)).
- **A new film on the home page.** "See it in two minutes" replaces the one-minute film: a tour of a made-up session, from the Sessions list and the Report's cover chart to Follow-up. It still loads only when played (`public/media/stormiq-tour.mp4`, about 10 MB).
- **The Sessions page, as a library.** Each row now shows the session's shape (the audience's chat per minute, as on its Report's cover chart), the share of attendees who wrote, its questions with how many are still open, and, under the title, what stood out most, in one line. A number the Report hides stays a dash, and the estimated ones say "est.". The rows' data is read as they come on screen, a few at a time (`GET /api/sessions/{id}/record/library`, [product.md](product.md#the-sessions-page-v1)).
- **Earlier versions and earlier outputs, on Follow-up.** Under each written output, "Earlier versions" lists the ones saved before it, newest first, with when and which model wrote them; each opens read-only with its own Copy. The Deep analysis, no longer made, is back read-only under "Earlier outputs" when one was saved for the session, with Copy and PDF (`GET /api/sessions/{id}/output-versions`, [api.md](api.md)).
- **What attendees wrote stays out of public formats.** The FAQ, Smart Recap and the summary, blog post, follow-up email and social posts no longer receive an attendee's name, an email, a phone number or a link, typed in a question or said on stage: StormIQ takes them out before the AI writes, and an output that still holds one is never saved ("The FAQ contained an attendee's name. Nothing was saved; please try again."). Speakers keep their names, and companies and products stay ([ai-outputs.md](ai-outputs.md#public-formats-what-attendees-wrote-redacted)).
- **Team digest.** Follow-up has a new item under Analysis: about 100 words to paste in the team's Slack or Teams channel, marked internal on its first line, with the session's key numbers, the busiest moments with their times, the questions still open and the decisions if they were listed. Haiku 5.5 writes it on click; StormIQ checks that every number and time in it is one it measured, or saves nothing. Nobody is named. French is a translation of the English one.
- **Decisions and commitments.** Follow-up has a new item under Analysis: what was decided on stage and who promised to do what, with the owner and due date when said, who said it, when, and the exact words. It is listed on click by Sonnet 5.5 from the transcript (no need to analyse the session first), and only what the transcript says word for word is kept. French is a translation of the English one.
- **Earlier versions of AI outputs are kept.** Writing an output again no longer loses the previous one: every saved output is kept with the models and prompts that made it, ready for the Follow-up page to show earlier versions. Nothing changes on screen yet.
- **Where each question came from, on the question desk.** Every question carries a coloured badge, "Q&A tab" in orange or "Chat" in blue, with an edge of the same colour, and a **Both · Q&A tab · Chat** switch beside the status filters keeps one source, with its counts.
- **Replay the session on the cover chart.** Moving the mouse across the chart under the Report's title (or a finger, or the arrow keys) follows the session minute by minute: a guide snaps to each minute, its chapter lights up with its full name, and a card shows the chapter, the messages and questions, what the speaker was saying and a few messages by name. The key moments sit on the ridge as numbered pins: hovering one tells what happened, clicking it opens its story under the Pulse. The chart draws itself in when the page opens, unless reduced motion is on.
- **Claude for every piece of text.** StormIQ no longer calls OpenAI for text: the analyses, Smart Recap, repurposed content and translations all run on Claude, and Admin no longer offers a provider or OpenAI models. A setting saved earlier that names OpenAI is ignored, and the job uses its Claude default. OpenAI is still called for cover images only. This also clears OpenAI's shutdown of its GPT-4 models on 2027-01-14.
- **What the room asked for.** "What stood out" now flags a topic several people asked about that mostly got no answer, with how many asked, how many are still unanswered and how much of the talk answered it, and in which chapter. A "Next time" line suggests answering it in the follow-up email. Marking questions answered on Follow-up updates it.
- **A navigation bar for each session.** Report, Transcript and Follow-up now open on the same bar, in the same place: "Sessions" and the session's title on the left, the three pages on the right, pinned to the top as you scroll. It replaces each page's breadcrumb and the tabs that sat lower on the Report, under its chart.
- **"In short" says what the Report found.** The first card now leads with the strongest findings, the moment the room reacted most and what it was reacting to, and the questions still open. "Checked, nothing unusual" is a set of tiles, always visible, each with its topic and verdict; the checks that couldn't run, and the audience parts with nothing to show, are no longer on the page but listed with their reasons in "Method and data quality".
- **Each moment of the Pulse, told.** Under "When the room reacted", every key moment the chart numbers now says what it was (the room answering a question from the stage, reacting on its own, greeting, or reporting sound trouble), what was being said, what people wrote, by name, and the questions asked then. A session with no stand-out minute tells its busiest ones.
- **Every question, and who spoke up.** "The questions" on the Report now lists every question, from the Q&A and the chat, still open then answered, with who asked, when, and the answer: written in Livestorm, yours, or the moment of the talk that answers it, quoted. Answering stays on Follow-up, one link away. In "The audience", "How spread out the chat was" becomes "Who spoke up": the people who wrote the most, by name and company, with how much they wrote and one of their messages. Names follow the same rules as the Transcript.
- **Add analyses the session.** "Add" now fetches the session, transcribes it and runs "Analyse session" on its own, while the Report shows "Preparing your report" with each step, then opens on the full Report. An analysed session no longer shows an "Analysed" card; the card appears only when there is something to run, a run in progress or a failure.
- **An event's sessions, a page at a time.** Opening an event on Add session reads its finished sessions 50 at a time, newest first, with "Show more", instead of every page of a large event at once, and no longer sends Livestorm's raw list to the browser. A line says how many have finished out of all the event has, since only finished ones can be added ("2 finished sessions of 6").
- **What stood out, in French, with what to do next time.** The findings card has an English · Français switch, for pasting into a report or an email. Each finding ends with a "Next time" tip from a curated library of event craft (wait ten seconds after asking the room something, run a sound check with every speaker, bring the room in every six to eight minutes…), never written by AI.
- **Clearer findings.** The chapter chart says what it shows (the room's own chat, in messages a minute, against the usual chapter), titles wrap instead of being cut, and sound or connection complaints no longer count as the room's interest: they belong to production friction. A finding no longer says "0 people" when the chat's authors couldn't be matched.
- **Where the room leaned in.** "What stood out" finds the minutes the audience wrote far more than its usual, on its own (not a reply to a question from the stage, not a wave of greetings), numbers them on the Pulse, and quotes what the speaker was saying at the time when the clock is checked to the second.
- **Monologues and prompts to the room.** "What stood out" now also finds a long stretch where one speaker talked without bringing the room in while the chat fell off (a band on the Pulse), and the questions from the stage the room answered only after the speaker had moved on, with how long they waited (a triangle under the Pulse's chat for every prompt).
- **What stood out.** The Report has a findings section, worked out by code from the session's own pattern. **Chapter by chapter**: which chapter drew the room in, or lost it, by the chat the audience wrote on its own (greetings, logistics and replies to a question from the stage don't count), with a bar per chapter against the usual one. **Production friction**: bursts of sound or connection complaints, with any silence on stage at the time, and a late start with the waiting room's messages. What was checked and found nothing, and what couldn't be checked and why, sit in a collapsed list.
- **FAQ.** Follow-up's Repurposing list has a FAQ: the audience's questions and their answers (written in Livestorm, yours from the question desk, or found in the talk with the time to watch in the replay), then the ones still to answer. Questions are reworded and merged, and who asked is never named. It is written on click by Haiku 5.5 once the session is analysed, in English, then French from the English one.
- **A cover chart that says something.** The chart under the Report's title is now labelled ("Audience chat per minute") and has a time axis. It marks the busiest minute, dots the minutes with questions and, once the session is analysed, lays the chapters underneath. Below it: the busiest minute and the chapter it fell in, the liveliest chapter (most chat per minute) and the chapter with the most questions.
- **"Analyse session" comes back when the session wasn't read.** A session whose chat was labelled before it had a transcript (or before StormIQ read sessions) showed "Analysed" while its chapters, located answers and Session analysis said to analyse it. The Report now offers "Analyse session" again (the chat isn't labelled twice), and "Analyse again" when the session changed since it was read.
- **Before you share.** Follow-up opens with the figures said on stage that may be wrong, when the analysis found some: an acronym the recording can't settle ("Was it NRR or NPS?") or the same metric said with two values. One click answers each; the Session analysis is told your answers and never states an unchecked figure as a fact, and the AI outputs say when a figure is still unchecked.
- **GIFs inside a sentence, and names on older chats.** A GIPHY or Tenor link now shows as a GIF under the message's words too. A chat saved before StormIQ read who wrote each message (before 5 November) named nobody: the Report says so and offers "Fetch again from Livestorm" (free; the transcript stays).
- **A Transcript that's easier to read and search.** The search, the view and the speaker filters sit right above the lines and stay pinned under the tabs as you scroll. Each speaker has a colour. When speech and chat are shown apart, the chat is a column beside the speech that stays in view, with its own search and a filter by author; when they share one clock, the author filter works on the whole timeline. Speech is in the same easier reading style as Follow-up.
- **Three question downloads.** The question desk's "Download (CSV)" offers the open questions, all questions with their status, or all questions with their answers (Livestorm's, yours, or where the talk answered them), ready for a follow-up email. Each names who asked, never with an email.
- **A better Session analysis.** It is now written by Sonnet 5.5 (it was Haiku 4.5) from what StormIQ measured and checked: the Report's numbers, the chat's busiest minutes and topics, the chapters, the questions and where the talk answered them, and the transcript, instead of the raw Livestorm data. Four sections: what happened, what landed with the audience, the questions, and what to do next. Numbers are quoted from the Report, never computed by the model, and names never reach it. It needs "Analyse session" first, and costs about $0.10–0.20 a run ([ai-outputs.md](ai-outputs.md#session-analysis)).
- **Follow-up, easier to read and to use.** AI text is set for reading: 16px in a softer ink, lighter in dark mode, with headings that clearly stand out from the text. "Overall analysis" is now **Session analysis**; the Deep analysis is off the list for now. The Smart Recap is one item with a **Formal · Hype** switch. Each social post, each follow-up email and the subject lines have their own **Copy**, which copies clean text.
- **Internal sessions read like any other.** The Transcript tab and the question desk show who wrote each message ("Priya S.") on internal sessions too, instead of "Team" everywhere (still never an email, and names never go to a model). The Report's ridge and the Pulse count everyone's chat as the audience's, as the measures already did, so an internal session's ridge is no longer flat.
- **GIFs in the chat.** A message that is only a GIPHY or Tenor GIF link shows the GIF on the Transcript tab and in the Report's message lists (loaded when scrolled into view, with no referrer). Any other link stays text.
- **A clearer "Analysing the session".** While the analysis runs, the Report shows its two steps (label the chat, read the transcript) with done, in progress and next marks, a progress bar, how long it has been running, and that it keeps going if you leave the page. The worker reports each step as it starts.
- **The session's tabs stay in view.** Report · Transcript · Follow-up pin to the top of the window as you scroll, so another tab is one click away from anywhere on the page.
- **Chapters.** Once "Analyse session" has read the session, the Transcript tab shows each chapter as a heading among the lines, and Follow-up has **Chapters and show notes**: "00:00 Title" lines on the recording's clock, ready to copy into a replay page or a YouTube description, with a note when YouTube wouldn't read them as chapters. No model call: they come from the analysis already stored ([product.md](product.md#the-follow-up-tab-v1)).
- **Questions answered in the talk, on the question desk.** Once "Analyse session" has read the session, a question nobody marked that the speaker seems to answer shows "Covered in the talk, not marked", with the time and the speaker's words. **Confirm** marks it answered here; **Not this** keeps it open and never suggests it again. A question marked answered aloud in Livestorm shows where in the talk. Passages are kept only when their words are in the transcript and come after the question. They stay in the open-questions CSV until confirmed ([product.md](product.md#the-follow-up-tab-v1)).
- **"Analyse session" now also reads the session.** After labelling the chat, the analysis makes one Sonnet 5.5 call over the session's transcript and questions (about $0.20–0.30) and keeps what code can check: the chapters, where a question was answered in the talk, the prompts to the room, the figures said on stage (flagged when the same metric has two values or an acronym like NPS/NRR is unclear) and the questions that repeat each other. The model sees ids and words, never a name. It is skipped when nothing changed since the last run, and when there is no transcript. Nothing on the page shows it yet: the question desk's "covered in the talk", the chapters, "Before you share" and the FAQ build on it next ([ai-outputs.md](ai-outputs.md#the-understanding-v1)).
- **The question desk, on the Follow-up tab.** The tab now opens on it: every audience question with who asked it, when, and its status (answered in writing, answered aloud, answered here, still open), filtered by Still open, Answered or All. "Mark answered" takes an optional answer; a question you marked can be edited or reopened. "Open questions (CSV)" downloads the open ones with who asked them. It needs no transcript and calls no model. Surprise me leaves the Smart Recap list for a small, opt-in card at the bottom ([product.md](product.md#the-follow-up-tab-v1)).
- **The question desk's server side.** `GET /api/sessions/{id}/record/questions` lists every audience question (the Q&A, and the chat's content questions once the session is analysed) with its status: answered in writing, marked answered aloud, answered in StormIQ, or still open. `PUT` on the same path lets the host mark one answered, with an optional reply, saved per question in the new `session_question_replies` table. `GET …/record/questions.csv` downloads the open questions with who asked them (name and company, on every session; no email). The Follow-up page above uses them ([api.md](api.md#the-question-desk)).
- **The classic stylesheet is gone.** `styles.css` (about 1,600 lines of the old dark design) no longer loads; the few base rules every page still relied on (box-sizing, the page's height, link and control resets, a default focus ring) moved into `tokens.css`, now the app's only global stylesheet. Code in the beta notice and in AI text is no longer blue: it uses the system's monospace in the text colour. On a phone, Admin's People and Sessions tables no longer widen the page.
- **Admin and the beta notice, rebuilt: every page is now a v1 page.** Admin has People (who has access, making and removing admins, each removal confirmed on its row), Sessions (every organization's, each opening its Report; delete asks again) and Models (provider, default and Smart Recap models, cover image model), with the same settings and model lists as before. The beta notice keeps its wording (its transcript row and its provider note updated), in v1 cards. The OAuth return page too. With no page left in the dark frame, the frame and the `v1Native` route flag are gone; the classic `styles.css` goes next.
- **The classic Overview and Chat & Questions views are gone.** The Report covers them (attendance, the room, the questions, the audience), and the Transcript tab lists every chat message and question. Their old links open the Report; Admin's session links open the Report too. With them go the classic chart cards, `DataTable`, the session hero and the SPA's own audience split (`utils/audience.js`), and the shell's older session tabs: every session page is now a v1 page.
- **Cross Analysis is out of the app.** The "In development" placeholder page is removed, and comparing sessions is off the roadmap for now, as `single-analysis` went from the URLs; `/cross-analysis` opens Sessions.
- **The Follow-up tab, rebuilt.** `/sessions/:id/follow-up` holds every AI output on one page: the overall and deep analysis, the Smart Recap in its three tones, and the summary, blog post, follow-up email and social posts. Each is written only on a click, in English or French (French once the English one exists), with its progress, then Copy and PDF. The model's Markdown is shown as text, never as HTML. The classic Analysis, Smart Recap and Repurposing views are gone; their old links open the matching output ([product.md](product.md#the-follow-up-tab-v1)).
- **Speaking stats on the Transcript tab.** A collapsed card with the pace (words a minute), the longest stretch and who held it, and the talk time per speaker with its share and pace, named as on the tab. Three new server-side measures, `m.speaking_pace`, `m.speaker_talk_time` and `m.longest_stretch` (`METRICS_VERSION` 7), gated by the transcript's health: hidden, and saying why, when too little of the recording was transcribed, when the speakers can't be told apart, or under a minute of speech ([product.md §13](product.md#speaking-pace)).
- **The Transcript tab, rebuilt.** `/sessions/:id/transcript` is now one readable timeline: what was said and what the room wrote, interleaved on the session clock when the recording is matched to it to the second, apart (and saying why) otherwise. Speakers are named once per stretch, unclear words are underlined, chat and Q&A lines carry their author's short name ("Priya S.", or "Team" / "Attendee" when the people list can't say), and questions answered in writing say so. Search, Everything / Speech only / Chat and questions, speaker filters, "Name speakers", subtitles (SRT) and the raw JSON. Without a transcript, the tab offers to make one (on a click only), follows it while it runs and offers "Try again" when it fails. The classic eight diagnostic tabs are gone; pace and talk time come back as a "Speaking stats" card ([product.md §6](product.md#6-transcript)).
- **Add session, rebuilt.** `/sessions/add` (`/search` redirects there) replaces the classic Search page and its three tabs. It lists your Livestorm events, each opening its finished sessions with their date, name and attendees. A session already added says so and opens; "Add" fetches a new one and opens its Report as soon as it is saved, while the transcript goes on. A failed fetch says why on its row. A session's or an event's link or ID can still be pasted, and a link is read for its ID. `POST /api/event-sessions` now also sends each session's `name`, `startedAt` and `attendees` next to its `label` ([product.md](product.md#add-session-v1)).
- **Simpler URLs: `/sessions`.** The Sessions page is now `/sessions` and a session's pages `/sessions/:sessionId/report`, `/transcript` and so on, instead of `/single-analysis/...`. Every link shared before still opens: `/single-analysis/...` redirects to the same path under `/sessions`, query and hash included (`src/sessions/paths.js`).
- **Signing in no longer stops on the "Connecting" screen, and it returns you to where you were.** After Livestorm's sign-in, the return page (`/auth/callback`) sometimes stayed on "Connecting with Livestorm" until you clicked Sessions: the app checked the router's route before the router had resolved it (the page's chunk was still loading), so it missed that it was on the return page. It now reads the browser's own address. The sign-in also goes back to the page you connected from (a shared session link opens that session), which it said it did but didn't: the callback adds that path as `next`, checked on the server (`oauth_client.safe_return_path`) and again in the app (`src/auth/callback.js`) so it never leaves the site. A failed sign-in returns to the home page. The Sessions table also shows attendees "53 of 57" on one line.

- **One StormIQ, for everyone.** The new design is no longer behind `?ui=v1`: everyone gets it, and the classic layout is gone (the sidebar, the classic card grid of sessions, the `?ui` switch and its saved choice, and every "Use the classic design" link). `/` is the home page for everyone, a session opens on its Report, and `/design` is open. The pages not rebuilt yet (Add session, the session's Transcript, Chat & Questions, Analysis, Smart Recap and Repurposing tabs, Cross Analysis, Admin) still open inside the new shell, in their older dark frame, until each is rebuilt. `?ui=v1` and `?ui=classic` in old links are ignored ([brand.md](brand.md#one-design)).

- **A home page, a promo film, and the wordmark's "IQ" as the favicon.** In the v1 design, the site's address (`/`) is now its home page: signed out, the landing page, without redirecting to `/single-analysis`; signed in, Sessions. The wordmark links home and signing out returns there. The landing page has a one-minute promo film ("See it in a minute") under its opening: a made-up session, captioned as such and as showing some of what is still being built; it loads only when played and never autoplays. The favicon and the app's square monogram now use the wordmark's own "I" and "Q" (Sora 600) on the blue disc, drawn from the font by `scripts/make_favicon.py`, instead of a hand-drawn approximation ([brand.md](brand.md#the-iq-monogram-and-the-wordmark)).
- **The Sessions page in the v1 design.** With the switch on, `/single-analysis` is no longer the classic card grid in a dark frame. It's a v1 page: "Sessions" with "Add session", three counts (sessions, with a transcript, events), a search, an event filter, a sort, and one table row per session: date, title (the event's, as on the Report), length, attendees of registrants, transcript ready or not, who added it. A row opens the session's Report. On a phone each row becomes a short block. Attendees are Livestorm's counts and the page says so. Wording in `src/sessions/list.js` ([product.md §4](product.md#the-sessions-page-v1)). Nothing changes with the switch off.

- **A landing page for visitors who aren't signed in.** In the v1 design, someone who isn't connected no longer sees the Sessions list's empty state and navigation they can't use. Any page shows a landing page instead: what StormIQ does, an example report with made-up numbers (labelled), what you get, how it works, why it can be trusted, and "Connect with Livestorm". The top bar keeps only the lockup, the theme toggle and the Connect button. The URL stays, so a shared session link returns to that session once connected. The beta notice and the design gallery stay open to everyone. Nothing is shown until the app knows whether you are signed in, so the page never flashes for a signed-in user. The classic design is unchanged ([product.md §3](product.md#3-authentication)).

- **The lockup everywhere, and an "IQ" monogram for square spots.** PDFs are now headed by "StormIQ | for [Livestorm logo]" (the official on-light logo, rendered unchanged from the brand kit, `livestorm_app/assets/`). The favicon (`favicon.svg`, `.ico`, the apple-touch icon), the collapsed classic sidebar and the compact top bar show a new "IQ" monogram in a circle instead of the three-bar mark ([brand.md](brand.md#the-iq-monogram-and-the-wordmark)).

- **Co-brand lockup in the headers.** The v1 top bar now reads "StormIQ", the Beta tag, then "for" and the official Livestorm logo; the StormIQ disc is gone from it. The expanded classic sidebar shows "StormIQ" with "for Livestorm" under it. The logo is the brand kit's file, on-light or on-dark to match the theme (`ForLivestorm.vue`, `--siq-logo-on-*` tokens). ## November 2026: all-hands fixes

- **Chat Messages counts the chat.** Session Overview's Chat Messages added up each person's `messages_count`, which Livestorm sometimes reports as 0 for everyone even when the chat has messages. It now counts the audience messages in the cached chat, as the Chat page does, and uses `messages_count` only when no chat is cached ([product.md §13](product.md#13-glossary-of-metrics-and-formulas)). The session summary sent to the AI counts the same way, an empty cached chat as 0. A person's own message count (People table, Engagement Score) still comes from `messages_count`.
- **Timelines with chat or questions alone.** Content Pace × Audience Activity and Segments With The Most Reactions were empty for a session with chat but no questions, or questions but no chat. A transcript and either source are now enough; the missing source counts 0.
- **Event session count.** The event list showed Livestorm's `sessions_count` as sent, a negative number included. A count that isn't a non-negative integer is left out of the event label and shown as "n/a".

- **The alignment probe.** `livestorm_app/record/alignment.py` works out where the recording sits on the session clock, so speech can be read against the chat. The job's arguments are ids only; the user's connection id waits on the sync's row until the worker claims it, and every other way out clears it too: a failed enqueue, a lost job found by the next status read, and the stuck-job sweeper, which now covers these rows. The worker checks that the connection belongs to the session's org before any Livestorm call, and only a member of the session's org can start one: an admin reading another org's session starts nothing. Guards keep it from spending an org's Livestorm quota: one sync per session at a time, none within 15 minutes of a completed one, and one per org at a time (a Redis lock that a second sync waits for; a third fails at once, so one org never fills the worker's job slots). A daily cap, `LIVESTORM_SYNC_DAILY_CALLS_PER_ORG` (default 1,000), counts the calls an org's syncs recorded in a rolling 24 hours, a session's first sync excepted: once reached, `roster-sync` answers 429 and a sync already queued fails before its first call; the sync running when it is reached finishes. Calls are counted per HTTP request, the HTTP session's retries included. A failed sync hands arq only its own message, never the `requests` exception, which carries the user's token and the upstream body. A first roster is due its refresh 7 days after the session ends. Nothing starts a sync automatically yet, and the v1 record still reads the people cached with the session; both come next ([jobs.md](jobs.md#people-sync), [api.md](api.md#the-people-sync)).
- **The Anthropic SDK carries every Anthropic call.** `llm_client` now calls Anthropic through the official `anthropic` SDK (1.x, which sends the same API version, `2023-06-01`), with one client per process given the key and the host explicitly. The SDK's own retries are off; StormIQ's retry policy is unchanged (429, 500, 502, 503, 504, 529 and connection errors, up to 4 attempts, `Retry-After` honoured, timeouts never retried), except that a connect timeout is no longer retried, since the SDK reports it as a timeout. The classic outputs send the same request bytes as before, `temperature` included, and never stream. While `ANTHROPIC_CUSTOM_HEADERS` is set, no Anthropic call is made, since the SDK would add those headers over the key and the API version. A failed call now fails the job with "The Anthropic call failed (HTTP 529 overloaded_error). Nothing was saved. Please try again." instead of the HTTP library's message ([providers.md](providers.md#the-anthropic-sdk)).
- **Streaming with a deadline.** A v1 call whose budget is over 16,000 tokens (the understanding job's 48,000) now streams instead of being refused. Its job's `call_deadline_s` (600 s) stops a stream that runs longer, on time even when the model sends only pings or nothing at all: a timer shuts the stream's own connection. A stream is retried only when it fails before its first event, and one that doesn't finish keeps nothing. Usage rows record whether a call streamed. Nothing runs the v1 jobs yet. OpenAI calls stay on `requests` and don't stream.
- **`scripts/llm_smoke.py --i-understand-this-costs`** checks with real, paid calls (well under a dollar) that the offered Claude models and the OpenAI rows accept the parameters StormIQ plans to send, and whether a cached prefix is read. It reads the keys from the environment, touches no database, prints acceptance, stop reasons and cache reads only, and exits 1 when a call was not accepted or a repeated prefix read nothing from the cache ([operations.md](operations.md#scripts)).
